07
Technical Philosophy

Build for where the
frontier will be.

Ratchet

7-month capability doubling commoditizes today’s frontier. Infrastructure must anticipate, not react.

Hybrid Cognition

Local routine processing, cloud for complex tasks. Sovereignty requires compute at the edge, not only the datacenter.

Exploration Architecture

Optimized for unknown discovery over known extraction. Knowledge creation, not content generation.

07 / 10 The Grove Foundation
Three architectural principles. First, the ratchet: capabilities double roughly every 7 months, which means anything built for today’s frontier is obsolete by deployment. We design for the next cycle, not the current one. Second, hybrid cognition: sensitive and routine processing stays local; complex tasks use shared cloud. This preserves institutional sovereignty. Third, exploration architecture: commercial AI optimizes for extraction (ad clicks, engagement). Grove optimizes for discovery — genuine knowledge creation that serves research and public understanding.
